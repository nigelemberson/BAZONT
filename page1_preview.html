Why Bazont Exists

Remote transactions can be risky.

Without protection, both buyer and seller
face a high level of risk.

1) Buyers don't want to send their money first.
2) Sellers don't want to send their goods first.
This creates deadlock.
DEADLOCK
...in the canyon of trade.

Bazont removes the deadlock by holding the payment securely.

Payment is released when the courier confirms delivery.
